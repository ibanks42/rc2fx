RC2FX's noise suppression weights (DeepFilterNet3, LA0 and LA1) were trained
by the RC2FX project on the datasets below. No dataset audio is included in
RC2FX. RC2FX releases the weights under the MIT licence; that does not
relicense these datasets, whose own terms still apply to them.

A licence is named only where it was checked. "See the dataset's terms" means
it was not verified here.

- DNS Challenge 4 (ICASSP 2022): fullband clean speech, noise and room impulse
  responses. Microsoft DNS Challenge. See the dataset's terms.
  https://github.com/microsoft/DNS-Challenge

- CSTR VCTK Corpus, as distributed with DNS Challenge 4. Centre for Speech
  Technology Research (CSTR), University of Edinburgh. See the dataset's
  terms. https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443

- AudioSet and Freesound clips, as distributed with the DNS Challenge 4 noise
  set. See the dataset's terms. https://research.google.com/audioset/ and
  https://freesound.org

- OpenSLR SLR26 (simulated room impulse responses) and SLR28 (room impulse
  responses and noise), as distributed with DNS Challenge 4. Apache 2.0.
  https://www.openslr.org/26/ and https://www.openslr.org/28/

- FSD50K, keyboard and click sounds. Each clip has its own Creative Commons
  licence. https://zenodo.org/records/4060432

- ESC-50, mouse click and keyboard typing classes. Karol J. Piczak.
  Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 (CC BY-NC 3.0), a
  non-commercial licence. https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50

- Freesound keyboard and mouse sounds: 796 sounds under CC0 1.0 and the
  156 sounds under CC BY 4.0 listed at the end of this file.
  https://freesound.org

- SKAID keystroke dataset. Benjamin Quattrone and Youakim Badr. CC BY 4.0.
  https://zenodo.org/records/17282184

- Keystrokes dataset. Pedro Pinto, Diogo Rafael Gonçalves Rodrigues, Gonçalo
  da Costa Macedo and Silvestre Malta. CC BY 4.0.
  https://zenodo.org/records/19453177

- Keyboard and click recordings. Yoshiyuki Kobayashi / Sony Group Corporation.
  MIT. https://zenodo.org/records/16564409

- About 14 typing clips, probably from an OpenGameArt keyboard pack (CC0);
  source not confirmed.

- Keyboard and mouse recordings by the RC2FX project author.
